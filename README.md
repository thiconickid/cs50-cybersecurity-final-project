## Project Overview
This final project for CS50's Introduction to Cybersecurity analyzes the Change Healthcare ransomware attack that occurred in February 2024. The presentation explores the architecture of the breach, the cascading impact on the US healthcare system, and key technical concepts learned throughout the course.

## Key Technical Concepts Covered
- **Authentication & Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** Analysis of compromised credentials and the critical importance of MFA on remote portals.
- **Ransomware Operations:** How ransomware encrypts data, disrupts critical infrastructure, and exfiltrates sensitive information.
- **Access Control & Least Privilege:** Restricting access rights to limit internal exposure and damage.
- **Network Segmentation:** Preventing lateral movement across enterprise networks.
- **Incident Response & Containment:** The trade-offs of taking systems offline during an attack.
- **Privacy & The CIA Triad:** Impact on Availability and Confidentiality affecting millions of individuals.
